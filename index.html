Sacred Laws

The Coven's Code

To maintain harmony and mystery within our sanctuary. Transgression shall be met with consequence.

I

Reverence

Respect is the foundation of our coven. Any form of disrespect, harassment, or discrimination shall result in immediate banishment from the sanctuary.

II

No Proclamations

External advertisements require prior blessing from the High Council. Unauthorized proclamations shall be met with swift exile.

III

Honor the Council

The Council's decrees are final. Disputes must be raised through proper channels with reverence. Disrespect toward Council members invites severe consequence.

IV

Sacred Secrets

Never reveal personal mysteries (yours or others). The sanctuary's security is paramount. No request for private information shall be tolerated.

V

Pure Speech

No hateful, discriminatory, or vulgar incantations. Our sanctuary is a space of harmony: keep your words clean and respectful.

VI

False Accusations

False reports and manipulation of the sacred judgment system shall be met with sanctions. The system exists to protect, not to be exploited.

VII

One Soul, One Form

One account per soul. Alternate forms, vague identities, or evasion attempts shall be detected and banished. Return after banishment = eternal blacklist.

VIII

Summoning Restraint

Spamming mentions (souls, Council, roles) is forbidden. A summoning must be justified and non-abusive. Spam results in immediate silencing.

IX

Voice Sanctum

No screaming, abusive soundboards, or disturbing noises. Clean voice, reasonable volume. Respect others' speech, no aggressive interruptions.

X

Shared Visions

Images, visions, and messages must remain pure. No shocking, gore, NSFW, illegal, or dangerous content. Links must be secure and verified.

XI

Forbidden Arts

Any discussion, promotion, or use of exploits, cheats, or glitches in the Roblox realm shall result in permanent banishment and report to the realm's architects.

XII

Consequences

Sanctions may include: warning, silencing, exile, banishment, eternal blacklist. Severity depends on behavior and repetition. The Council reserves the right to act.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the mysteries that trouble your mind

How to join the Council?
You must be active, respectful, and devoted to the community. When recruitment opens, a sacred form appears in the dedicated channel. The High Council examines your attitude, maturity, and reliability before choosing you.
How to report a realm anomaly?
Use the bug-reports channel or open a ticket in the "Bug Report" category. Provide as much detail as possible (visions, steps to reproduce the anomaly) to help our architects.
How to obtain a special role or Beta Tester status?
Special roles are granted based on your activity, devotion, and behavior. The Beta Tester role is usually awarded during open testing phases or to reward the most devoted community members.
How to report an issue with a Council member?
Open a ticket in the "Appeal" or "Council Issue" category. Explain calmly what occurred and add proof if you have any (visions, logs). The situation will be reviewed by the High Council.
Is the realm accessible on mobile and console?
Yes, Your Fish Life is crafted to be fully compatible on PC, Mobile, and Console (Xbox, PlayStation), so every soul can enjoy the experience, regardless of their vessel.
