01 — The Game

Deep water,
shallow learning curve

Six systems, one loop. Everything in Your Fish Life is designed to reward patience rather than grind — the tank grows while you slow down.

01 / BUILD

Tank construction

A grid-based editor with hundreds of decorations, substrate layers, filtration units and lighting rigs. Every placement affects water chemistry, and every chemistry change affects what survives.

02 / SPECIES
100+

Distinct fish, each with temperament, depth preference and breeding constraints.

03 / GENETICS

Breeding

Trait inheritance, mutation rolls and lineage tracking. Rare morphs are earned, not bought.

04 / ECONOMY

Trading

Verified player-to-player exchange with escrow. No blind trades, no exploits.

05 / EVENTS

Seasonal drops

Rotating quests, limited species and community milestones tracked live on Discord.

06 / MODERATION

Enforced, not decorative

Every action is logged with an ID, a reason and a paper trail. Infractions can be revoked, appeals are reviewed within 48 hours, and the whole audit stream is public to staff.

07 / CADENCE

Weekly builds

Active development with a shipped changelog every week. Beta access is granted to contributors first — testers shape the patch, not just report it.

03 — Rules

Twelve
lines

Short enough to read, specific enough to enforce. Every sanction carries an ID and can be appealed through a ticket.

01

Respect is the floor

Harassment, hate speech and discrimination end in a permanent ban. No warning cycle applies to targeted abuse.

02

No unsolicited promotion

Servers, channels, projects and external links need written staff approval. Scam or phishing links are an instant removal.

03

Staff decisions stand

Disagree through a ticket, never in public channels. Appeals are reviewed by a second moderator within 48 hours.

04

Privacy is absolute

Never post personal information — yours or anyone else's. Staff will never request passwords or payment details.

05

Keep language clean

No slurs, no graphic content, no shock posting. The server is readable by teenagers and adults alike.

06

Report in good faith

False reports and attempts to weaponise the moderation system are themselves infractions and are logged as such.

07

One account per person

Alternate accounts used to evade sanctions result in a blacklist across every associated identity.

08

Mentions are not a tool

Ping with a reason. Mass-mentioning staff or roles triggers an automatic timeout.

09

Voice discipline

No screaming, no soundboard spam, no deliberate interference. Push-to-talk if your environment is loud.

10

Content stays safe

No NSFW, gore, illegal material or unverified executables. Links must be inspectable and safe to open.

11

Exploits are off-limits

Sharing, promoting or using game exploits results in a permanent ban and a report to the development team.

12

Sanctions are recorded

Warn, timeout, kick, ban, blacklist. Each entry is timestamped, attributed and revocable by the issuing authority.
