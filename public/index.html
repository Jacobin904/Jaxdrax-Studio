About the Game

Your Fish Life is an upcoming Roblox experience developed by Jaxdrax Studio. Build your dream aquarium, discover unique fish species, and relax in a peaceful underwater world. Our game focuses on creativity, exploration, and community interaction.

Key Features

Aquarium Building

Design and customize your own aquarium with a wide variety of decorations, plants, and tank styles. Express your creativity and build the perfect underwater home for your fish.

Fish Discovery

Discover and collect new fish species as you explore different environments. Each fish has unique behaviors and requirements, making every collection special.

Community Events

Participate in regular community events, competitions, and seasonal updates. Connect with other players and share your aquarium designs.

Relaxing Gameplay

Enjoy a peaceful gaming experience with no pressure or time limits. Play at your own pace and create the aquarium of your dreams.

Regular Updates

We are committed to providing regular content updates, new fish species, decorations, and features based on community feedback.

Cross-Platform

Play on PC, mobile, or console. Your progress is saved across all platforms, so you can enjoy Your Fish Life anywhere.
