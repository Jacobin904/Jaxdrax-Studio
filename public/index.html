Key Features

Discover what makes Your Fish Life a unique and engaging experience that will captivate you for hours

Aquarium Building

Design and customize your own aquarium with a wide variety of decorations, plants, and tank styles. Express your creativity and build the perfect underwater home for your fish with unlimited possibilities.

Fish Discovery

Discover and collect new fish species as you explore different environments. Each fish has unique behaviors and requirements, making every collection special and rewarding to maintain.

Community Events

Participate in regular community events, competitions, and seasonal updates. Connect with other players and share your aquarium designs with a vibrant and supportive community.

Relaxing Gameplay

Enjoy a peaceful gaming experience with no pressure or time limits. Play at your own pace and create the aquarium of your dreams in a stress-free environment designed for relaxation.

Regular Updates

We are committed to providing regular content updates, new fish species, decorations, and features based on community feedback. Your voice matters in shaping the future of the game.

Cross-Platform

Play on PC, mobile, or console. Your progress is saved across all platforms, so you can enjoy Your Fish Life anywhere, anytime, on any device you prefer.
